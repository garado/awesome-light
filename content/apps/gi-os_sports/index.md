Follow a club or a whole league. You get one column of scores, newest at the top, and a notification when a game you follow starts or ends.

Fourteen leagues: MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL, MLS, WNBA, NWSL, PWHL, WPBL, Formula 1, and the four levels of minor league baseball.

The data comes from public JSON endpoints that need no key, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to paste in. Tap a game for the box score, the venue and the TV listing. A second tab has standings for the majors.

LightOS has no Play Services, so this is a plain sideloaded APK. Every push to the repo publishes a signed release, which Obtainium can track.
