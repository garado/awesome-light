Roll replaces the stock Camera and the stock Album. It is a rewrite, not a fork. You can set it as the default camera, so the hardware camera button opens it.

The roll sits above the viewfinder. Pull down and your photos come into view, newest first against the top-right corner. Older ones run up and to the left, the way a contact sheet reads. Flick up from anywhere and you are back at the shutter. That is the whole navigation model.

What it does:

- Filters, with an optional date back stamped in the corner
- Film-roll mode: a fixed frame count you shoot through before you can look
- PRO, video, selfie and QR modes
- Face detection, run on the hardware
- The wheel works as a lens ring for zoom and exposure

The panel is grey in normal use. Roll lifts the grey while you look through the viewfinder, which is why these shots are in colour.

Light did not make this app. It installs as a plain APK and needs no root. Uninstall it and the stock camera is as it was.
