Pick a beat frequency from 1 to 50 Hz, labelled from deep sleep to energized focus, then set the tone and volume and mix in white, pink or brown noise if you like. Includes a monaural mode for the speaker, a sleep timer that fades out, and keeps playing with the screen off. Works offline with no internet permission.
