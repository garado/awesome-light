Search for a stop by name, road, or stop code, or use Nearby to find stops around you. Save frequently used stops and see up to three live arrival estimates for each service.

Live arrivals require your own LTA DataMall API Account Key, which you enter in Settings. The app stores the key on the device and sends it to LTA over HTTPS. Nearby uses location on the device to sort stops by distance.
