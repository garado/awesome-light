An online radio player for the Light Phone 3.

Fork of [mrrobashcroft/radio-tool](https://awesome-light.garado.dev/apps/ashcroftrob_radio), developed and verified on a real Light Phone 3 (LP3). MIT licensed.

## Features

- **Now Playing**: scrolling station/track info, center play/stop, favorites star.
- **Background playback**: `LightAudioService` (media3/ExoPlayer) with foreground notification.
- **Find Stations**: search with Radio Browser or play a direct stream URL.
- **Library**: Display favourite stations and recently played stations.
- **Bluetooth**: Displays current Bluetooth status. Tapping opens system settings.
- **Volume**: The volume buttons adjust the active stream (media if playing, else ringer).
