A minimalist audiobook player built with the Light Phone SDK. This tool lets users listen to their own audiobook library on the device and pick up exactly where they left off.

## Features
- **Local Library**: Plays books from the device's Audiobooks folder — no account, no cloud, no subscriptions.
- **Background Playback**: Keeps playing when the tool is minimized or the screen is off, with playback controls in the notification.
- **Chapter Navigation**: Reads embedded chapters from MP3 and M4B files, with per-chapter progress and instant chapter picking.
- **Playback Speed**: Adjustable from 0.5x to 2x with a single global setting.
- **Listening Memory**: Remembers the position of every book, resuming with a short rewind to re-orient.
- **Minimalist Aesthetic**: Pure monochrome design following the Light Phone III design language.
