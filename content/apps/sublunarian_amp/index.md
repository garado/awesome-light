A music player for your own library — Navidrome, Subsonic, Plex, Jellyfin, or files on the phone.

Amp streams and downloads from a server you run, or files on the phone. Downloads are kept for offline listening, and it falls back to them on its own when the connection goes. Synced lyrics, casting to a DLNA speaker, playlists, likes and ratings all work against the server they came from.

Built on Light's SDK, in the Light aesthetic: no artwork unless you
want it, optional color, and an A–Z index down the side of every long list. The library is three buttons at the bottom by default — library, search, now playing.
