Three public-domain translations bundled — King James, World English Bible and
Berean Standard — with every verse on the phone. No permissions, no network
calls, nothing to sign in to.

Tap a verse to highlight it, or long-press and drag to mark individual words.
Full-text search across the translation. Tapping a search result or a highlight
opens on that verse, not the top of the chapter.

MIT, 8.5MB.
