Light Tickets keeps the original ticket PDF or image, including event details, seating information, and extra pages. Send a file from a phone or laptop browser on the same trusted private Wi-Fi, approve Save on the Light Phone, and open the saved copy offline.

- PDF, PNG, JPEG, and WebP support.
- Multi-page PDF viewing, pinch and double-tap zoom, Fit, and a full-screen view with a visible Back button.
- No account, ads, or analytics. Free and open source under the MIT license.

**Community beta:** version 0.4.3 was built and signed by Light using SDK 0.2.7. [Download the beta](https://github.com/izzystst/light-tickets/releases/tag/v0.4.3) and follow the [sideloading instructions](https://github.com/izzystst/light-tickets/blob/main/SIDELOADING.md).

Tested on a physical Light Phone III, including wireless upload, offline reopening, navigation, removal, and a successful iPhone scan of the displayed practice QR with USB unplugged. Venue admission has not been tested. Static tickets only; rotating barcodes, provider logins, NFC admission, and payments are not supported.

Wireless transfer uses **unencrypted HTTP**: use trusted private Wi-Fi. Android backup exclusion remains unresolved, so saved tickets may be included in backups or device transfer. See [known issues](https://github.com/izzystst/light-tickets/blob/main/KNOWN_ISSUES.md). Screenshots show only the dummy practice ticket.
